The manifesto / 2072 to 2076

A future you can build.

Read the 2072 manifestoPDF / 32 pages / Opens in a new tab

A new mandate must be earned.

In 2068, voters sent the LCA into opposition. Four years later, we are asking to return with a new programme: limits on state power, a fair chance to get ahead and a government you can trust.

A different direction.

Liberty under law.

Judicial warrants, independent intelligence oversight and an end to blanket seventy-year secrecy. The same limits will bind our own ministers.

A fair chance at work.

Secret workplace ballots, voluntary unions and shared leave insurance. Protect workers while opening the door to new hires and new firms.

Lead CU reform.

Keep our place in the Columbian Union. Seek transparent spending, practical market access and stronger parliamentary scrutiny.

Start with honest government.

Introduce liberty and integrity bills in the first 100 days. Publish an independent opening audit and the funding conditions for every expansion.

Build a life here.

A place to call yours.

Aim for 50% more annual home completions by 2076 than the verified 2072 total. Release serviced land and build near jobs and transport.

Back the next good idea.

Replace compulsory workplace arrangements with voluntary unions and secret ballots. Protect pay and safety from day one, with fair probation rules.

Talent deserves a chance.

Excellent teaching, strong reading and mathematics, and help before a student falls behind.

Care, when it matters.

More primary care capacity and clear access standards, including for communities far from a hospital.

Keep life moving.

Let clean generation, storage and demand response compete on cost and reliability. Clear grid queues for projects ready to build.

Your life. Your freedom.

Introduce a Liberty Restoration Bill in the first 100 days. Protect free expression, privacy and equal citizenship without tests of political loyalty.

An open country. A steady partner.

Secure sea lanes and open markets. Make Caprica a partner others want to trade and build with.

Ready to protect our home.

Prioritize the navy, air force, maritime surveillance and the protection of vital cables and ports.
