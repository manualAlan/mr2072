Enterprise

Back the next good idea.

A small business is somebody’s big idea. A first job is somebody’s first chance. Both deserve room to grow.

A fair chance at work.

Replace compulsory workplace arrangements with voluntary unions and secret ballots. Protect pay and safety from day one, with fair probation rules.

Share the cost of family leave.

Develop pooled parental and serious-illness insurance with published contributions and benefit limits. Protect accrued rights through the transition.

Make room for new firms.

One-day standard incorporation, public invoices paid within 20 working days and a route to challenge licensing rules that shelter incumbents.

Pay for promises honestly.

Retain the earnings-credit principle. Publish an opening fiscal audit, cost permanent commitments and set a path to falling debt relative to GDP by 2076 under normal conditions.
