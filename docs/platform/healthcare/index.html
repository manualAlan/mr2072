Healthcare

Care, when it matters.

When you are unwell, getting help should be the simple part.

An easier first appointment.

More primary care capacity and clear access standards, including for communities far from a hospital.

A waiting-time right.

Arrange suitable accredited alternative care at no extra charge when a commissioner misses a clinical maximum wait. Put staffing and funding in place first.

Mental health without the maze.

By 2074, aim for first clinical contact within 14 days for urgent nonemergency referrals under 25. Publish treatment waits too; emergencies need immediate assessment.
