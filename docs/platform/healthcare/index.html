Healthcare

Care, when it matters.

When you are unwell, getting help should be the simple part.

An easier first appointment.

More primary care capacity and clear access standards, including for communities far from a hospital.

A shorter wait.

Use available public and independent capacity. Universal access should mean treatment, not another queue.

Trust the people who care.

Give clinicians time with patients. Publish actual waiting times and listen when care falls short.
