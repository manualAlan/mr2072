Civil liberties

Your life. Your freedom.

Government holds power on your behalf. It should remember who that power belongs to.

Restore the limits on power.

Introduce a Liberty Restoration Bill in the first 100 days. Protect free expression, privacy and equal citizenship without tests of political loyalty.

Keep intelligence under law.

Judicial warrants, accountable human authorisation and no intelligence-controlled army. Preserve the Nuremberg Act framework and prohibit torture.

End blanket secrecy.

Review classified records every five years, with a presumption of release after 20 and narrow, independently reviewed extensions. Give watchdogs access to the files and budget.

A person must answer.

Guarantee human review of consequential automated public decisions. Protect encryption and keep essential services accessible without mandatory digital identity.
